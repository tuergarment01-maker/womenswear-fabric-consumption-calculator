How this estimate works

Woven bases at 145 cm usable width: Mini Dress 1.3 m; Midi Dress 1.8 m; Maxi / Resort Dress 2.4 m; Blouse / Shirt 1.2 m; Wide-Leg Linen Trousers 1.6 m. Metres per piece = base × 145 ÷ selected width × (1 + material buffer). One yard = 0.9144 metres. Totals use unrounded values; displayed values are rounded.

Illustrative knit bases: 3GG 450 g, 5GG 420 g, 7GG 390 g, 9GG 360 g, 12GG 320 g, 14GG 300 g, 16GG 280 g, plus 5% yarn loss. These gauge assumptions are planning scenarios, not measured specifications: yarn count, stitch, size and construction can change weight substantially. Fabric width does not apply to knitted garments.

The combined buffer is a simple cutting / shrinkage planning allowance, not a tested shrinkage guarantee or a complete marker efficiency model. Size mix, bias cutting, prints, nap, lining, defects and pattern layout may need more material. Confirm consumption after pattern and sample approval. Material cost excludes labour, trims, lining, testing, packing, freight and taxes; it is not a finished garment unit price. Production timing starts after relevant approvals and material readiness.

Lining and grading methodology

Shell consumption is multiplied by 1.11 for the extended-range scenario. Full lining uses 100% and half lining 50% of the base shell consumption, adjusted for the lining width, the same grading factor and a separate 4% lining cutting/shrinkage allowance. Shell shrinkage is not applied again to lining. Cotton voile is a planning default for lightweight/sheerness scenarios; blazers default to Poly/Viscose twill. Confirm material suitability and lining panels with your sample. Skirts and blazers require your own base pattern consumption; no factory consumption is invented. This is a planning estimator, not CAD nesting or a substitute for a marker.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

One colour requires 200 pieces per style. Two or more colours require at least 150 pieces per colour per style. Up to four sizes can be mixed.

Does this calculate a finished garment price?

No. Enter a fabric price per metre or yarn price per kilogram to estimate material cost only. Labour, trims, lining, testing, packing, freight and taxes are excluded. Request a factory quote for the finished garment.

Are shrinkage and yarn weight guaranteed?

No. The buffers and gauge-based weights are illustrative planning assumptions. Confirm actual consumption, shrinkage and yarn weight with the approved pattern, material tests and sample.

What are the production lead times?

Sampling takes 5–10 working days. First bulk orders take 30–45 working days; repeat orders take 15–30 working days, after relevant approvals and material readiness.

What is the sample fee policy?

Sample fee is 3x estimated unit price for new clients, 100% refunded once your bulk order is placed.

GSM to fabric weight

GSM means grams per square metre. Weight does not replace a pattern marker or determine opacity.

Net textile kg = metres × width in metres × GSM ÷ 1000. Excludes roll cores and packaging.
